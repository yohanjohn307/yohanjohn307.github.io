## Course Description

Mathematical modeling and analysis of linear dynamical systems arising in mechanical and electromechanical engineering. Topics include Laplace transforms, transfer functions, block diagrams, state-space representations, natural and forced response, stability analysis, and frequency-domain methods. Emphasis on deriving and interpreting system response from physical models of translational, rotational, and electrical systems.
